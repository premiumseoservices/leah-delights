FAQ

Questions? We've got answers

How far in advance should I order?

+
We recommend ordering at least 1–2 weeks in advance for custom cakes, and 3–4 weeks for wedding cakes and large orders. The earlier you order, the better — especially for popular dates like holidays and school breaks!

What flavours do you offer?

+
We offer a wide range of flavours including vanilla, chocolate, red velvet, lemon, strawberry, caramel, and many more seasonal flavours. We can also cater for dietary requirements — just ask us!

Do you deliver or is it pick-up only?

+
We offer both pick-up and local delivery options. Please contact us for delivery availability and pricing based on your location. For large or multi-tiered cakes, we may hand-deliver to ensure they arrive in perfect condition.

Can I provide my own design inspiration?

+
Absolutely! We love it when customers bring inspiration images, Pinterest boards, or their own sketches. The more information you share with us, the closer we can get to your dream creation.

How do I pay for my order?

+
We require a deposit to secure your booking date, with the balance due before collection or delivery. We'll confirm payment details when you place your order. We accept bank transfers and various mobile payment methods.

What if I need to cancel my order?

+
We understand life happens! Please contact us as soon as possible if you need to cancel or reschedule. Deposits are generally non-refundable as they cover preparation costs, but we'll always try to work with you where possible.

Do you cater for allergies or dietary needs?

+
Yes! Please always inform us of any allergies or dietary requirements at the time of ordering. We'll do our best to accommodate, though please note our kitchen does handle common allergens such as nuts, dairy, eggs, and gluten.

How do I store my cake once I receive it?

+
Most cakes can be stored at room temperature for 1–2 days in a cool, dry place, away from direct sunlight. Cakes with fresh cream or fruit fillings should be refrigerated. We'll provide specific storage instructions with every order.
